## description

In Empathify, users can form lobbies, and within the lobby there are three to five game rounds.
1. One round is proposing a song to the audience. User choose the song that the others must rate.
2. User win the game when the others rate the song with a high rank (values from 0 to 10 are possible).
3. After three rounds, a user has proposed three songs, and it's a chill game with hanging out with friends while listening to the music.
4. After each song, the users must rate the song.
5. After every song is played, you see who has the best rating, who knows the audience best, and who has the most empathy for the group.

## challenge

We have no traffic yet. We have no ads, and we have no traction in this project, although it's a good game that needs to be refurbished. It's not UI and UX optimized, and we have not tested the stripe monetization.
