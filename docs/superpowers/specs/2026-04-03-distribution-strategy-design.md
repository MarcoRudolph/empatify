# Rudolpho-AI Distribution Strategy

## Overview

**Company brand:** Rudolpho-AI — one studio, multiple products that bring people together.
**Approach:** Product-specific audiences targeted independently. Rudolpho-AI is the company identity, not the content destination.
**Budget:** Near zero — time + AI leverage only.
**Lead product:** Empatify (launching in ~1 week as of 2026-04-03).

### Products & Audiences

| Product | Audience | Primary Channel | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empatify | Friend groups, music lovers | TikTok, Reddit (r/spotify, r/indiegaming) | empatify.de |
| DateTalk | Couples, daters | TikTok couples niches, r/dating | datetalk.de |
| Rudolpho Chat | Creators, influencers managing DMs | Twitter/X creator community | rudolpho-chat.de |

## Phase 1: Launch Sprint (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** 500 real Empatify users.

### Tactic 1 — Product Hunt Launch

- Launch Tuesday–Thursday (peak PH traffic days)
- Find a known Hunter to feature Empatify — social proof boosts visibility
- Write the **first comment yourself** within minutes of going live (origin story format)
- DM your network personally with exact launch time — do NOT ask for upvotes publicly (PH bans this)
- Respond to every comment within the first 2 hours — PH algorithm rewards comment velocity
- Prepare all assets ahead of time: tagline, screenshots, 60-second demo video

### Tactic 2 — Reddit Community Drops

Target subreddits: **r/spotify**, **r/indiegaming**, **r/partyplanning**, **r/AskReddit**.

Rules:
- Never lead with a link. **Story first, link in comments** (or only if rules allow)
- Frame as personal anecdote: *"Built this after a house party argument about music taste — tried it with friends last week, here's what happened"*
- Post a GIF or screenshot of actual gameplay, not a landing page
- Engage hard in comments for 48h after posting — Reddit rewards comment velocity in ranking

### Tactic 3 — TikTok/Reels Micro-Influencer Seeding

- Find 10–15 creators with 5k–50k followers in **music reaction**, **friend group**, and **party/social** niches
- Use Modash (free tier) or manual TikTok search: `#spotifygames`, `#musicreaction`, `#partyideas`
- Give them free Empatify Pro access
- Send short DM pitch — **no script**, let them create naturally
- Reshare their content on @RudolphoAI
- UGC without a script outperforms branded content consistently

### Tactic 4 — Twitter/X Ship-in-Public

Post under **@RudolphoAI** — the company brand, not a product account.

Three content types:
1. **Origin story post** — launch week, aim for shares: *"I built Empatify because…"*
2. **Gameplay moments** — real leaderboard screenshots, song reveals, surprising results
3. **Music psychology hooks** — *"People who pick slow songs win 30% more rounds in Empatify — empathy beats taste"*

### Tactic 5 — Email Capture from Day 1

Add simple email capture to **rudolpho-ai.de**: *"Get notified when DateTalk launches."*

- Every Empatify visitor becomes a potential DateTalk beta user
- Owned audience = most valuable long-term asset
- Start building before DateTalk is built

## Phase 2: Compound Play (Months 2–3)

**Goal:** Passive discovery traffic via SEO + AEO. Works while you sleep.

### Tactic 6 — 10 AEO/SEO Landing Pages

**Domain split logic:**
- Product-specific pages → product domains (inherit existing backlink authority)
- Generic discovery / multi-product pages → rudolpho-ai.de (builds brand domain authority)

**Page structure per AEO playbook (HubSpot/Evergreen):**
- Question as H1 — matches the search query exactly
- Direct answer in the first paragraph — AI crawlers extract first clear answer
- Short paragraphs, tables, bullet lists
- Internal link to product for conversion
- FAQ schema markup

**10 target pages:**

| URL | Domain | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| `/games/spotify-party-game` | empatify.de | "spotify party game online" |
| `/games/music-game-friends` | empatify.de | "music games to play with friends online" |
| `/compare/jackbox-alternative` | empatify.de | "jackbox alternative free" |
| `/games/spotify-quiz-multiplayer` | empatify.de | "spotify quiz game multiplayer" |
| `/games/first-date-games` | date-talk.de | "games to play on a first date" |
| `/games/icebreaker-couples` | date-talk.de | "icebreaker games for couples" |
| `/games/get-to-know-you-game` | date-talk.de | "get to know your partner games" |
| `/tools/ai-instagram-dm-agent` | rudolpho-chat.de | "AI agent for instagram DMs" |
| `/games/social-games-online-2026` | rudolpho-ai.de | "social games online 2026" (spans all products) |
| `/about/ai-social-games-studio` | rudolpho-ai.de | "AI social games studio" (brand positioning) |

All 10 pages AI-generated in one session using a template prompt. Build time: ~2 hours.

### Tactic 7 — Twitter Content Pillars at Scale (AI-Assisted)

Three weekly content pillars, batch-generated with AI, 15 posts in 30 minutes:

| Pillar | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Gameplay moments | Screenshot + caption | Leaderboard reveal, surprising song pick |
| Music/social psychology | Fact hook | "People who pick slow songs win 30% more rounds — empathy beats taste" |
| Build in public | Milestone update | "Just hit 500 users. Here's what worked and what didn't." |

### Tactic 8 — Rudolpho-AI Newsletter

Monthly email to everyone captured via rudolpho-ai.de:
- What shipped across all products
- What's coming next (DateTalk teaser, Rudolpho Chat update)
- One behind-the-scenes story

Converts passive visitors into warm audience for every future launch.

## What's Explicitly Out of Scope

- Paid ads — no budget
- Separate domains per landing page — fragments authority, hurts SEO
- MCP for agent traffic — ahead of its time for consumer products; revisit when Rudolpho Chat has agent-native use case
- .info or cheap throwaway domains — spam-flagged, zero trust signal
